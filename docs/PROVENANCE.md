# Dataset provenance

| Dataset | Source | V1 status | Participants completed | Exclusions |
|---|---|---|---:|---|
| PhysioNet EEG Motor Movement/Imagery Database (EEGMMIDB) | PhysioNet | Evaluated | 105 | Participant 86: 24 eligible trials, below the 30-trial minimum |

V1 used runs 4, 8, and 12 and the T1/T2 event mapping implemented in
the project.

The exact download date was not recorded in the benchmark metadata.
Dataset access and reuse remain subject to the terms stated by the
official dataset provider. LeakCheck does not redistribute raw EEG
recordings.

The benchmark requested 106 participant IDs; 105 completed. This
number describes the V1 analysis set, not the total size of the
underlying PhysioNet database.

OpenBMI was not evaluated in V1. No multi-dataset or multi-site
validation claim is made.
